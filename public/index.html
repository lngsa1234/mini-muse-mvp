Connecting to Claude…

Ideas

Ideas for things you might want to do. Starter ideas appear instantly from simple rules. Then Muse reads your memory, goals and connected services and replaces them with personal ones. Tap Do it and Muse gets started; skip what doesn't fit and its next ideas get better.

Goals

Each goal has a plan Muse writes and works through. Tick steps yourself, or let Muse work on them while you do something else.

Suggested goals

Let Muse work on your goals
Muse reviews every goal, does the steps it can without you, and leaves you a short progress brief in Chat. Real Muse does this on its own after you close the app.

Library

Documents and notes Muse can search and cite. Add anything—meeting notes, a syllabus, an article—and Muse reads from it and tells you which document an answer came from. Muse can also add and remove documents for you.

    Connectors

    Connect a service to give Muse new tools. Each connector adds its own tools, and you decide whether Muse must ask before it changes anything. Disconnect anytime and the tools disappear.

    These are practice connectors filled with sample data, so everyone can try them safely. Nothing here touches your real accounts. Real Muse, and Claude itself, connect to real services the same way, through a standard called MCP.

    Settings

    Control what Muse remembers, review everything it has done, and choose how it answers.

    Memory

    What Muse knows about you. Muse adds to this when you tell it something lasting. Remove anything, anytime, or tell Muse “forget that” in Chat.

      Activity

      Every tool Muse used, every approval you gave, and every change you made, newest first.

        The agent loop

        Each reply runs this loop. Muse reads your message plus what it knows, picks a tool, reads the result, and repeats until it can answer.

        1. 1Reads your message and decides what to do
        2. 2Uses a tool: its own or from a connector
        3. 3Stops and asks you before changing anything
        4. 4Writes the reply
